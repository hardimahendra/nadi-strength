# Nadi Strength — Design Direction

Primary style: Typography-first; supporting styles: Industrial and Energetic. Layout: story-led grid. Signature: concentric training rings which turn the principle of progressive overload into a visual element.

Palette source: ID 62: `#012F25`, `#FAF2A0`, `#FC7D14`, mapped directly to `--night`, `--paper`/`--lime`, and `--clay`. Display uses Arial Black/system sans; editorial notes use Georgia. Motion is minimal and reduced-motion safe. Avoid: generic fitness photography, neon glow, and body-transformation claims.
